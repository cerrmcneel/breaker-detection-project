New Tool: Generate REBT-Compliant Single-Line Diagrams Instantly

July 14, 2026 • 3 min read
📐

We just shipped the Generador Unifilar, a browser-based CAD tool for producing single-line diagrams (esquemas unifilares) that comply with Spanish REBT/Industria requirements — no desktop software required.

The Problem Every Técnico Knows

Anyone who has prepared documentation for a boletín eléctrico knows the pain: redrawing the same panel schematic by hand, hunting for the right IEC symbol, then re-exporting when a client asks for one more circuit.

What It Does

The Generador Unifilar uses a UNE-EN 60617 symbol library, lets you edit values in real time, and exports straight to vector PDF or PNG — ready to attach to a technical file or hand to an Administrador de Fincas.

Two Ends of the Same Compliance Job

It's built on the same philosophy as our breaker-board Analizador IA: take a task that technical offices redo manually every week and make it instant and standardized. Combined with the panel photo analyzer, PanelSafe now covers both ends of a compliance job — auditing what's installed, and documenting what should be installed.

Try it at panelsafe.cv/unifilar — feedback from installers and técnicos is what shapes what we build next.
